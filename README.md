## 📖 About the Project

**ThriftCampus** is a campus-focused marketplace concept designed to help college students buy and sell useful second-hand items within their student community.

Instead of letting useful items go unused, students can give them a second life by passing them on to other students who need them.

The project focuses on making student-to-student exchanges more convenient, affordable, and sustainable.

## 🎯 Our Mission

- 💰 Make useful items more affordable for students.
- ♻️ Encourage reuse and reduce unnecessary waste.
- 📚 Give textbooks and study materials a second life.
- 🤝 Help students connect with buyers and sellers in their college community.
- 🚲 Make it easier to exchange items such as books, calculators, and bicycles.

## ✨ Planned & Implemented Features

The project aims to support features such as:

- 🛍️ **Item Listings** — Display items students want to sell.
- 📚 **Student Essentials** — Support listings for textbooks, calculators, bicycles, and other useful items.
- 💬 **Student Communication** — Help interested buyers and sellers connect.
- 📢 **Announcements** — Share relevant marketplace updates.
- 🚩 **Reporting** — Provide a way to report inappropriate or suspicious listings.
- 🏫 **Campus-Based Exchanges** — Encourage convenient handovers within the college community.
- 🎓 **College Community Access** — Explore restricting participation to verified college students.

*Feature availability depends on the current implementation. Planned features may require additional development.*

## 🌱 Why ThriftCampus?

Student life can be expensive, and many useful items are left unused after a semester or academic year.

ThriftCampus explores a simple idea: **one student's unused item can become another student's essential.**

By encouraging reuse within the campus community, the project aims to make student life more affordable while supporting a more sustainable way to exchange everyday items.
